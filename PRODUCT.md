# Black Wall Sticky Note World

## Product

A quiet, black-wall gallery where rooms hang like personal keepsakes and notes feel handwritten, tactile, and safe to revisit.

## Users

People collecting thoughts, plans, memories, and small creative fragments. They should understand the lobby and pin a first note without instructions.

## Emotional goals

- Peaceful before productive
- Warmly personal, never childish
- Tactile and collected, never dashboard-like
- Focused, with motion that rewards attention rather than demands it

## Core loop

Open lobby -> find or create a room -> enter a wall -> pin, write, arrange, revisit.

## Design guardrails

- Black wall remains the stage; color belongs to paper, pins, and small accents.
- Use real interaction affordances and accessible labels, not decoration disguised as controls.
- Prefer one meaningful effect per surface.
- Respect reduced motion and touch input.
- Do not add component-library styles that fight the paper, thread, and tape material language.
