# Decisions — October 2, shared-room redesign

1. **One room, two scenes.** The same night wall, arched moon-over-water window, existing fairy lights and CSS wood desk stay mounted through phrase acceptance. Preserve all existing lobby, room, note, chime and match functionality.
2. **Wick is the messenger.** One original cream candle spirit, drawn inline, with an amber flame and subtle waiting/shy/happy poses. No image request. User-requested doodle/handwritten details are intentional exceptions to generic design-skill bans.
3. **Paper inside glass.** The invitation uses existing night/glass language; its secret-code strip and opened letters are opaque cream paper. Reuse Caveat and existing UI sans; scoped colors follow the supplied amber/cream/rose palette. Do not alter global typography or palette.
4. **Short opening.** After decryption, a 650ms invitation departure and 450ms room arrival form a ~1.1s sequence. Reduced motion removes travel/loops and makes typing immediate. Six transient hearts/sparkles are removed after three seconds.
5. **Main letter and artifacts.** First note receives a sealed, clickable envelope. Read-note IDs/timestamps remain session-memory-only; folding/reopening or re-unlocking within the mounted session skips repeat typing. Photos, cassette-like audio, video frames and confirmed links retain real snapshot data. Mobile tall papers stay unrotated to avoid clipping.
6. **Privacy beats refresh convenience.** Close/Hide discard decrypted data and invalidate asynchronous work. Refresh requires the original link or encrypted file again because the URL fragment is intentionally cleared. No plaintext, phrase, key or read-history persistence was added.
7. **Reply is an existing flow.** Leave one back opens the visitor's archive, then focuses its composer. The user keeps and explicitly shares the reply; the app has no recipient address or direct-send backend.
8. **Identity.** An amber flame inside a rose heart wax seal on navy connects the match, candle messenger and private notes. Used in the lobby/shared door, SVG favicon, 192/512 PNG manifest icons and 180 Apple icon.
9. **Two distinct letter effects.** Local note text settles through CSS, and the root bug was clearing its piled-ID set on both toggle directions. Restore closed-note IDs on enable. The new shared-room ambient envelope layer owns one RAF, bounds objects to eight, and resets on OFF, hidden tab or reduced motion.
10. **Prior work stays intact.** Initial ItemPrivacy changes, the room-motion QA browser-selection change and unused letter-courier.png are not part of these commits. The earlier in-progress shared redesign and toggle correction are incorporated within their requested scope.

11. **Physical seal variant (October 3).** Keep the dark tile only in app identity/navigation. On envelopes and paper, use the bare wax heart. Combine SHARED-01/02 because the entry and opened room share components and state; keep icon and lifecycle commits separate.

12. **Refined identity and motion.** User rejected the heart badge and adjacent lobby branding. Replace it with a moonlit folded note, remove the lobby icon, and use a separate outline envelope for Share. Master audio wording is Sound in lobby and rooms. Envelope opening now visibly lifts the flap/insert before real message reveal; folding is a faster reverse departure. Timers cancel on unmount, repeat taps are guarded and focus follows the material.
13. **Share beyond localhost.** Localhost/loopback preview shares target https://krithi-1304.github.io/STICKY_WALL_WORLD/shared/. Hosted installations retain their own origin/base. Only encrypted content enters the fragment. Verified live public decryption with a synthetic UI-created share; no deployment is required to make new links usable. Existing localhost links must be regenerated.
