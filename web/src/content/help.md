# Your guide to The Black Wall

A quiet place to keep thoughts in rooms, or let them go. Use the topic search to find a control, a task, or a passphrase question. You can download this guide to read offline.

## Start here: keep your first thought

1. On your first visit, choose a passphrase of 12–200 characters and enter it twice. Use Show passphrase to check your typing. Store it somewhere you can find again.
2. Choose Create private archive. This creates an encrypted archive in this browser, on this device.
3. In the lobby, write in What is on your mind? Choose Keep it, select an existing unlocked room or name a new one, then choose Keep this thought. A blank new-room name becomes My thoughts.
4. Open the paper on the room wall to continue writing. Changes save automatically; wait for Saved encrypted on this device before leaving.
5. Choose Export encrypted backup and keep the downloaded file somewhere safe. Make a new backup after important changes.

The lobby draft is not saved until you keep it. Refreshing, leaving the lobby, or hiding the archive discards that draft. Choose Let it go if you want to discard it deliberately; you can cancel the confirmation.

## Open, hide, and protect your archive

On later visits, enter your archive passphrase and choose Unlock archive. A wrong passphrase does not erase your notes. Check your typing with Show passphrase and try again.

Choose Hide screen, or hold Escape for about half a second, to show a blank page and lock the archive. Return takes you back to the unlock screen after saving finishes. A short Escape press closes the current dialog instead.

There is no account, public feed, or cloud sync. Notes and attachments are encrypted in this browser's storage. Another browser, browser profile, or device has a separate archive. An unlocked screen or a compromised device is not protected by the passphrase. Dimmed light is an atmosphere control, not an encryption lock.

## Find, create, rename, and delete rooms

1. In the lobby, open a hanging room tag to enter it. Your rooms are ordered by their latest update.
2. Use Search rooms to filter the gallery by room name. If nothing matches, choose Create room with that name, or Show every room to clear the search.
3. Choose New room, type a name of up to 60 characters, then choose Hang it up. Back returns to the lobby.
4. Inside an unlocked room, select its title to rename it. Press Enter or move focus away to save; Escape cancels.
5. Use Let go below a room in the lobby to delete it. The confirmation includes every note in that room. Cancel keeps everything. Confirmed deletion has no undo; an earlier backup is the recovery option.

Locked rooms require their separate item passphrase before you can read them. Their names are replaced with a concealed label while locked.

## Write, edit, move, and revisit notes

Choose Pin a thought inside a room to create a note and open its editor. Select any unlocked note to open it. Only one note editor is open at a time.

Write in Your thought. Notes support up to 10,000 text characters. Edits save automatically. Done, Close note, or a short Escape closes the editor and keeps the note. When Falling letters is on, letters gathering at the bottom of the paper are a visual effect; your writing remains saved. Open the note again to read it.

Previous note and Next note move through the room's editable notes and skip notes with extra locks. Rooms hold up to 200 notes; create another room if one is full.

On wider screens, drag a note by its tape to adjust its position. With a keyboard, focus its tape and use the arrow keys. Movement is limited to a small area around its place on the wall. Small touch layouts keep the notes in a readable grid instead of dragging them.

Choose Let this go in the editor, or Let go on the note, to request deletion. Nothing is removed until you confirm. Cancel returns to your writing. Closing the editor alone never deletes a note.

## Format writing and add safe links

Select text in the editor and choose B for bold or I for italic. You can also type two asterisks around bold text or one around italic text. The formatted preview appears below your writing.

Choose Add link, enter a complete address beginning with https:// or http://, then choose Insert link. The link becomes part of your note. Opening an external link asks for confirmation before leaving for that website. Other address schemes are rejected. HTML code is treated as text rather than executed.

## Add photos, audio, and video

1. Open a note and choose Add photo, Add audio, or Add video. You can also use Attach photo, audio or video.
2. Select a file from your device and wait for Reading file to finish. Each note can hold up to three attachments.
3. Photos appear in the note. Audio and video have playback controls. Keep the editor open until the file has finished loading and the archive reports that it is saved.
4. To remove a file, choose Remove beside its name and confirm. Cancel keeps the attachment.

Supported formats are JPEG, PNG and WebP photos; MP3, WAV, Ogg and WebM audio; and MP4 or WebM video. The browser must be able to decode the file. A filename extension alone does not make a file valid.

There is no app-imposed file-size or duration limit. Browser storage, available memory and device performance still limit what can be saved. Large files can take time to process, back up and share. Attachments are included in your encrypted archive and backups.

## Add a heart lock to a note or room

1. Choose Lock note in the note editor, or Lock room in the room or gallery controls.
2. Choose a separate item passphrase of 12–200 characters, repeat it, then choose Lock item. Remember this passphrase separately from your archive passphrase.
3. To read the item later, choose Unlock note or Unlock room and enter its Item passphrase. Unlock item opens a temporary, read-only private view. The saved item stays locked.
4. Choose Close and keep locked when finished. Hiding or refreshing also closes the private view.

To edit a locked item, check Remove this lock so I can edit before submitting its passphrase, then choose Remove lock. This permanently removes the extra lock. Use Lock note or Lock room again when you want that additional protection restored.

Locking a room keeps any separate note locks inside it. Those notes remain concealed in the room's private view. Remove the room lock to access their individual unlock controls. There is no forgotten-item-passphrase reset. Locked items are excluded from all shared snapshots.

## Share a note, room, or space privately

1. Choose Share note, Share room, or Share space for the content you want to send. Space means the archive's shareable rooms and notes.
2. Enter and repeat a new Share passphrase of 12–200 characters. Choose Prepare private share.
3. Check the count of excluded locked items. For a small snapshot, choose Copy link. You can also choose Download shared file.
4. Larger snapshots, including large media, use an encrypted file instead of an oversized link. Send the file to your recipient without changing it.
5. Give the recipient the share passphrase separately from the link or file. For a file, also tell them to open the site's /shared page and select Open shared file.

Sharing creates a read-only copy at that moment. It does not enable collaboration, automatically update, or sync your archive. A copy already sent cannot be revoked by changing or deleting the original. A share passphrase is separate from your archive and item passphrases.

Links only work for other people when the site is hosted at an address they can reach. A localhost or 127.0.0.1 preview link is only for your own device. No content is shared automatically.

## Open a shared snapshot

Open the protected link from the sender, or open the site's /shared page and use Open shared file to select their encrypted file. Enter the Share passphrase provided separately and choose Open shared snapshot.

The snapshot is read-only and is not imported into your own archive. You can read its notes and play supported media. Hide screen clears the view. Return lets you open it again. A shared file is different from a full archive backup: use /shared for shared files and Choose an encrypted backup on the archive entry screen for backups.

## Export and restore a backup

1. While your archive is unlocked, choose Export encrypted backup. Check that the downloaded JSON file was saved. It includes your current rooms, notes, attachments and extra item locks.
2. Keep the file somewhere safe outside this browser. A backup is a snapshot; it does not update itself.
3. To restore it, use Hide screen and Return to reach the archive entry screen, or open the site in another browser. Choose an encrypted backup and select the file.
4. Enter the archive passphrase that protected that backup when you exported it, then choose Restore backup.
5. If an archive already exists here, restoration asks to replace it. Export that archive first if you want to keep both. Restoring replaces rather than merges archives. Cancel restore returns to ordinary entry.

Malformed backups and wrong passphrases are rejected. Do not clear browser data to troubleshoot an import problem: that can delete your current archive. Extra heart locks still need their own item passphrases after restoration.

## Change or recover a passphrase

While unlocked, choose Change passphrase, enter a new archive passphrase of 12–200 characters twice, and choose Save new passphrase. Keep the screen open until the operation completes. Your notes remain in place. Export a new backup afterward: older backup files still need the old passphrase. Changing the archive passphrase does not change item-lock or share passphrases.

If you forgot your archive passphrase, check your password manager. If the archive is still open in another tab, keep it open and use Change passphrase there.

If every tab is locked, choose Forgot passphrase? There is no way to decrypt old notes without the original passphrase. A backup also needs the passphrase used when it was exported.

To begin again, download the old encrypted archive first and verify the file was saved. Then enter a new passphrase twice, type START FRESH, and choose Replace with empty archive. This replaces the archive in this browser with an empty one. Keep the old file in case you remember its original passphrase later. Back to unlock cancels this process.

## Lights, chimes, depth, and keyboard controls

Chimes are off by default. Choose Sound off to enable them; the button becomes Sound on. Choose it again to mute. The preference is remembered in this browser. With sound enabled, room tags play a quiet chime when you hover or focus them, and controls have subtle material sounds. Touch does not play hover sounds.

The room's Light on / Light off control changes the wall lighting. In the dark, bring the pointer's match near a note to reveal it, or focus or tap the note to read. Fairy-light switches adjust the decorative lights. A small mood symbol can appear over dimmed paper; it is a simple word-based hint, not an assessment.

A crescent moon and six sparse stars sit above softly drifting night clouds. Pointer movement gives the atmosphere and paper a small perspective shift. Clicking controls provides depth feedback. Your system's Reduce motion setting simplifies these effects while keeping the controls usable.

Each room has a Falling letters on / off button. Turn it off to keep words in their normal positions when you close or dim a note, and to close the editor without the falling-letter delay. Turn it on to bring the letter pile and reassembly back. This choice is saved separately for each room, survives locking and refreshing, and is included in encrypted backups. Existing rooms start with the effect on. Confirmed note deletion still removes the paper; this setting does not change what is saved or deleted.

Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between controls, Enter to follow a room link or submit a form, and Space or Enter to activate buttons. Arrow keys move focused note tape. Escape closes a dialog; holding it hides the archive. Help closes back to the control you used to open it, without leaving your draft.

## Troubleshoot saving, storage, and access

Saving encrypted means a write is in progress. Saved encrypted on this device confirms the latest successful save. If you see Not saved — retry or export, keep the tab open, export an encrypted backup, then try Retry saving. Do not assume unsaved changes will survive a refresh. If export also fails, keep the tab open while making space available on the device.

Only one tab can edit this archive at a time. If you see an another-tab message, return to the already-open tab, or hide or close it before unlocking here. This prevents one tab from overwriting another's changes.

Browser data is local. Clearing it, losing the device, or closing a private-browsing session can lose the archive. Keep encrypted backups outside the browser. If a room seems missing, clear Search rooms and check that you are using the original browser profile and site address.

If a media file fails, check its format and whether your browser can play or display it. If a room is full, create another room. If a share cannot be opened, check whether it is a shared file or an archive backup and use the correct screen and passphrase.

If writing brings up something difficult, the find someone to talk to link beneath the writing area opens support resources, with confirmation before visiting an external site.
