A very dark black-blue night sky with delicate, natural, softly moonlit charcoal clouds drifting across the bottom quarter and outer left/right edges. The top half and middle 60 percent are mostly uncluttered near-black negative space for cream handwritten UI text. Subtle silvery edges on a few cloud wisps, soft analog photographic grain, velvety tonal depth, romantic and contemplative. Restrained near-black, charcoal, muted midnight blue, traces of warm ivory illumination. The cloud shapes should look organic and beautifully photographic, not abstract gradients or painted wave lines. No moon and no stars IN THIS TEXTURE: the website will add one crescent moon and exactly six tiny animated stars separately. No horizon line, no water, no mountains, no buildings, no text, no UI, no frames, no patterns, no neon, no purple nebula, no bright blobs. Keep the edges naturally dark so the image blends into a black page.
